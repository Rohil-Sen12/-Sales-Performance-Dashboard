#  Sales-Performance-Dashboard

The Sales Performance Dashboard provides a comprehensive and interactive view of sales data, helping businesses monitor revenue, profit, and order trends. It enables stakeholders to analyze product performance, customer behavior, and regional sales distribution, supporting data-driven decisions to improve sales efficiency and business growth.

Key Insights

1.Overall Sales Performance: Tracks total sales, profit, and order count

2.Sales Trend Analysis: Monthly and yearly comparison of sales growth

3.Top & Bottom Products: Identifies best-performing and underperforming products

4.Category-wise Sales: Highlights revenue contribution by product categories

5.Region-wise Analysis: Shows geographic sales performance

5.Customer Insights: Identifies high-value and repeat customers

6.Profitability Analysis: Evaluates profit margins and discount impact.
